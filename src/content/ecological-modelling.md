A treatment that kills mites in a dish is not the same as a treatment that saves
a colony. This model is about the gap between those two statements.

## The question

What level of efficacy, applied how often and for how long, changes the
trajectory of a colony under mite pressure, and where the thresholds sit.

## Inputs and assumptions

Every parameter, its source and its uncertainty, in one table. Assumptions get
their own rows; hiding an assumption inside a parameter is how models become
unfalsifiable.

## Limitations

What the model cannot represent: reinfestation from neighbouring colonies,
seasonal variation, beekeeper behaviour, and anything else that would move the
answer materially.
