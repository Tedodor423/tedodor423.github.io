A laboratory result is not a product, and iGEM is not the end of a project that
would need years of work to reach a hive. This section is about what would have
to be true for NECTAR to matter outside a university lab, and it is written as
a plan rather than a promise.

## What we would do next

The honest short list. Not "future directions" in the sense of everything one
could imagine, but the specific things that would have to happen next, in order,
and why each is next.

The deck closes on three, which is the right number:

- **Optimise targets**: more candidates, and more than one at a time
- **Validate efficacy**: the mite work the project did not reach
- **Scale production**: titre, and the fermentation route behind it

A fourth sits in the design section of the deck: developing a **multimeric
therapeutic**, hitting several targets at once as resistance management. That is
a design ambition rather than a next step, and belongs under _what would have to
be true_.
