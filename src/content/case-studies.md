> **What this page proves:** that "would it work?" has a different answer in
> different places, and that we designed against that rather than around it.

The same technology meets very different realities. A treatment that is
affordable in one beekeeping economy is not in another; a regulatory route that
is open in one country is closed in the next; and the pest pressure itself
differs enough to change what a useful treatment would even look like.

Rejecting the idea of a single universal deployment context is one of the
conclusions of [our human practices work](/human-practices), and these pages are
where that conclusion is worked out.

## Where the losses are

Run the years and the picture is not one epidemic spreading outward. It is a
band of countries reporting steady losses, one country pulling away from the
rest, and a continent that only starts counting in 2023.

```component
varroa-map
```

Three things in that figure set up the case studies. The United States climbs
from 36% of colonies lost to 55.6% over the run. Australia has three years of
data and no more, because the mite only arrived in 2022. The United Kingdom has
a single measured year, and every other year drawn for it is an inference from
the European average.

## How each case study is written

Each region is its own HONEY cycle, framed around one question: _how would the
requirements for NECTAR change here?_

| Beat            | What it covers in a case study                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **H: Hear**     | The pest situation as people there describe it                |
| **O: Observe**  | Current practice, costs, constraints, regulation, attitudes   |
| **N: Navigate** | What an acceptable treatment would have to do in this context |
| **E: Evaluate** | What that implies for NECTAR specifically                     |
| **Y: Yield**    | What we changed, and what the region still leaves unresolved  |

Kept to that shape, each case study is a page. Left unshaped, each becomes a
dissertation nobody reads, which is the failure mode to avoid here.
