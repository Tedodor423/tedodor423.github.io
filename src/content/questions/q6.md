## Hear

Le Feuvre anchors Australia — recent establishment, reinvasion, treatment
constraints, beekeeper economics. Lewis, Hiatt and Goodrich anchor
California — migratory commercial beekeeping, almond pollination,
operational scale. The Scottish Government team put Varroa in Scotland's top
three bee threats, which places the problem outside our two case studies
entirely.

## Observe

The same product meets different constraints in each place: formic acid's
temperature ceiling and the missing brood break in Australia, honey heated
to 65 °C for eight hours to enter Western Australia, about 80% of US
commercial colonies converging on one almond bloom, and a regulatory answer
that is genuinely different in each jurisdiction.

## Navigate

We rejected the assumption that there is one deployment context, and built
the regions out as separate case studies along five axes: pest pressure,
current practice, economics, regulation and stakeholder attitudes.

## Yield

Region-specific hive profiles and country-specific economic analyses,
regional efficacy and honey-processing tests in the wet lab, and a
deployment argument that does not assume one market. The open loop: none of
the beekeepers who would use NECTAR has seen it work, because we do not yet
have efficacy data to show them.
