---
title: Why RNAi rather than another chemical?
hear: lam*, budge*, le-feuvre, hiatt, us-regulators, colin, mcloughlin
---

## Hear

Lam set out the charges against chemical pesticides — non-target toxicity,
bioaccumulation, resistance — and the standard for the alternative:
off-target screening against justified representative species, and risk
handled as identify, assess, manage, communicate. Budge answered the
question that could have sunk the route: mites feeding on the fat body do
ingest haemolymph, so dsRNA fed to bees can reach them.

## Observe

Australia and the US both approve synthetic miticides and organic acids, and
both watch resistance erode them; chemical controls therefore sit at the very
top of the IPM pyramid. RNAi is not new either — the US EPA has already
registered Calantha and Norroa. Norroa is also where its shortcomings show:
aimed at mite reproduction, so it takes weeks, and fed as a sugar syrup to
workers rather than to the larvae Varroa feed on. So ours has to be better
than the existing RNAi product, not merely different from a chemical.

## Navigate

Three requirements came from the comparison with Norroa: target genes that
kill mites directly, find a route that reaches larvae, and make the delivery
commercially scalable. Specificity became a further design criterion on Lam's
advice.

## Evaluate

A new mode of action resistant mites have never met, modular because a
different sequence silences a different gene, and biodegradable — dsRNA has
been reported undetectable in soil within two days. Specificity is a design
parameter rather than a property to accept, which is also why it has to be
demonstrated rather than asserted, and why Q4 exists as its own question.

## Yield

Targets chosen for mite survival rather than reproduction; off-target
screening moved inside the RNA design pipeline rather than after it; and, as
the criteria accumulated, one sequence became a platform for designing them.
Next question: does the thing that makes and delivers the RNA have to be alive
in the hive?
