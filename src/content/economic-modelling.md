## Methodological discipline

The part most teams skip, and the part a judge will notice. Two commitments:

- **Counterfactual reasoning.** The benefit is the difference from what would
  have happened anyway, not the total value of the activity.
- **No multiplying a whole industry by an efficacy figure.** It produces
  impressive numbers and means nothing. We refuse it explicitly and say why.
