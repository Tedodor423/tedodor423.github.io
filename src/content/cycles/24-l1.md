# First DBTL — pilot testing the dosage for larval assays

**Question.** dsRNA needs to be administered to larvae to assess stability of dose
over the period of capping — the point at which the workers seal the cell over with
wax.

**Design.**

- Larval rearing very difficult.
- Insufficient time to train in larval rearing.
- Consider in-hive dosing method.
- Consider regulations around administering nucleic acid to a hive — see
  [safety and security](/project-safety).
- Discussion with Prof. Geraldine Wright.
- Consulted leading entomologists → agreed in-hive dosing was the best approach given
  the timeframe.

**Build.**

- One construct only: **700 bp GFP dsRNA**, with no biological silencing target,
  made by IVT and checked by Qubit and gel before dosing.
- 5 µL of 200 ng/µL — a **1 µg total dose** — diluted 4× with 15 µL of sucrose
  solution to give a 20 µL dose per larva.
- Controls received 20 µL of sucrose alone, from the same batch as the treatment
  group.
- All larvae staged at **5th instar** at the moment of dosing.

**Test.**

- Feed dose to larvae; return to sample at each timepoint.
- Five timepoints — 24 h, 48 h, 72 h, 96 h and 7 days — with **10 dosed larvae and
  5 sucrose-only controls** at each.
- Test for presence using RT-PCR.

**What we learnt.**

- Larvae eat administered dose.
- dsRNA present following feeding.
- In-hive administration feasible.
