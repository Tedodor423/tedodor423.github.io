# Does the model tell us anything about the state we are modelling?

**Question.** Can BEEHAVE, unmodified, reproduce a colony under mite pressure well
enough for us to build on?

**Design.** Baseline runs establishing use of the model. BEEHAVE couples colony
demography, foraging and mite population dynamics in one agent-based model, so a
treatment can be judged **against colony survival** rather than against mite counts.
We extended it with a treatment layer of our own rather than writing a colony model
from scratch.

**Build.** Stock BEEHAVE, no treatment layer.

**Test.** Baseline colony trajectories.

**Result.** The cycle produced results and is mostly written up, but in the team's
working document rather than here.
