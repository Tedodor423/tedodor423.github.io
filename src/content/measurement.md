This page carries more weight than most. Measurement is where this project has
the strongest claim to having built something reusable, and it is also where the
most instructive mistake was made and corrected.

## The measurement problem

Total RNA concentration tells you almost nothing useful here. What matters is
how much of a _specific_, _intact_, double-stranded molecule is present in a
messy biological matrix, and standard methods answer a different question. Set
that up clearly, because every decision below follows from it.

## What exactly are we measuring

Define the measurand properly: what molecule, in what state, in what material,
expressed in what units. A method that cannot state this is not a measurement
method.

## Controls

Listed explicitly, because a measurement page without its controls is an
assertion:

- Untagged or tag-free RNA
- Dye or reagent blank
- Matrix blank
- No-template and no-reverse-transcription controls
- Positive control at known concentration
