The dated record of the bee lab, plus the protocols in enough detail for another
team to repeat them. [The bee lab page](/bee-lab) is the argument; this is the
record it rests on.

## How this page is organised

Two halves. **Protocols** first, because that is what a future team comes here
for. **The dated record** second, because that is what a judge comes here for.

Entries keep their original dates. Where an entry records something that did not
work, it stays; the corrected version goes underneath rather than replacing it.

## Protocols

One subsection per protocol, each written so that someone who has never done it
could follow it: materials, steps, timings, and the failure modes we hit.

> **PDF:** Full protocol set, uploaded to `static.igem.wiki` and linked here
> for download. The page carries the summaries; the PDF carries every step.

### Colony and bee handling

### Feeding and dosing

### Larval work

Staging, extraction and handling.

### Sample preparation

Haemolymph and tissue collection, storage and preservation before analysis.

### Mite handling

Collection, husbandry and survival.
