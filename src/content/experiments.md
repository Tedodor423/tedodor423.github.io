Protocols in enough detail for another team to repeat them, and the dated record
of what we actually did. [Wet lab](/wet-lab) is the argument; this is the
evidence and the method behind it.

## How this page is organised

**Protocols** first: written to be followed, not admired. **The dated record**
second, with its dates intact.

Protocols here are the versions we ended up using. Where an earlier version
failed, the failure is recorded in the dated entries and summarised in
[Engineering](/engineering) rather than quietly deleted.

## Protocols

> **PDF:** Complete protocol set, uploaded to `static.igem.wiki` and linked
> here. This page carries working summaries; the PDF carries every step.

### Nucleic acid preparation

Purification of DNA and RNA, with the practical notes that are not in the kit
instructions and that cost us time to learn.

### Assembly and cloning

The assembly strategy step by step, plus screening.

### Transcription

The production pipeline, its timings, and what changes yield.

### Quantification and quality control

How material was measured and checked, and what each method can and cannot tell
you. See [measurement](/measurement) for the methods development behind this.

### Yeast work

Transformation, culture and induction. See [yeast](/yeast).

### Quantitative PCR

Written in our own voice, including the parts that are judgement rather than
procedure: replication, melt-curve interpretation, controls, and the realities
of working with negatives that are not always clean.

This is the single most reusable protocol on this page and is a
[Contribution](/contribution) in its own right.
