The question this page answers is narrow and useful: _what did Oxford 2026 save
the next team from having to rediscover?_

## How each contribution is written

Three lines each, no more:

1. **What it is**: one sentence.
2. **Why another team would want it**: the problem it saves them.
3. **Where to get it**: a link, a part number, a protocol, a file.

A list of nouns is not a contribution. A thing someone else can pick up and use
is.

each of those. This is worth more as a handbook than as notebook entries.
